# Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the order date. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Orders that were already refunded cannot be refunded again.

# Approval
Every refund requires approval by an authorised approver before it is executed. Refunds above EUR 500 require approval by an administrator.

# Outside the refund window
Requests outside the 30-day window are declined; a goodwill account credit of up to EUR 50 may be offered with approval.
