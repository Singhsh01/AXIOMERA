# Response targets
P1 cases must receive a response within 1 hour, P2 within 4 hours, P3 within 24 hours and P4 within 72 hours. Enterprise customers receive half these targets, premium customers three quarters.

# Escalation
When a response target is breached, the case is escalated to the team lead and the on-call channel is notified. A second breach escalates to the head of operations.

# Outages
Production outages affecting multiple customers are P1. The SRE on-call engineer opens an incident ticket and posts to the SRE alert channel immediately.
