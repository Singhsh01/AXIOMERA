# Company overview
Helix Ridge Software GmbH was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Munich with a second delivery centre in Leipzig. Helix Ridge employs 140 people, of whom 95 are engineers.

# Focus
Helix Ridge builds and operates HelixFlow, a configurable case management and workflow platform for public sector organisations, utilities and insurers. HelixFlow digitises citizen and customer requests end to end: intake, routing, case handling, approvals, correspondence and reporting.

# Experience
Helix Ridge has delivered more than 30 case management implementations in Germany and Austria since 2016. The company has worked with municipal administrations, energy utilities and insurance carriers.

# Delivery model
Projects are delivered by cross-functional teams of product owners, solution architects, engineers and change managers. Delivery follows an agile, iterative methodology with fixed governance checkpoints.
