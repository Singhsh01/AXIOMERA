# Stadtwerke Talheim — customer service case management (2023)
Implemented HelixFlow for the customer service department of a regional energy utility. 450 caseworkers, migration of 1.2 million historical cases, delivered in 9 months. Average case handling time fell after go-live.

# Rheinmark Insurance — claims intake automation (2024)
Automated first notice of loss intake for motor and household claims, including document classification and routing. Delivered in 7 months with integration to the core claims system.

# District Office Aldersee — permit application workflows (2022)
Digitised 14 permit and licence procedures for a district administration, including citizen portal, online payment and electronic file. Delivered in 11 months; WCAG 2.1 AA audited.
